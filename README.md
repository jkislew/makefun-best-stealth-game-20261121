NIGHT LEDGER — a top-down stealth game. Wren, a young archivist in a teal hood, slips through a moonlit archive to steal back the stolen Night Ledger: bend 9 patrolling guards with thrown coins, cross 3 archive rooms in shadow, and escape unseen. Guards have real vision cones clipped by walls and tall cover, gradual detection meters with "?" and "!" awareness, ghost markers at last-seen positions, and hearing that pulls them to footstep rings and coin clinks. Shadow halves detection, marble rings out under walking feet, and every coin throw is aimed with a full preview — arc, landing marker, and the exact noise ring it will make. Five ledger pages gate the exits, and taking the Ledger from the Vault Hall starts an escape phase back across the hall through alerted patrols. Graded at the end: Ghost, Shadow, Phantom, or Night Clerk, with 8 feats to earn. No libraries, no network, no build step — plain ES modules and a canvas, with a WebAudio synth for every cue.
